About the studio

A room built for women to take up space.

ZEVA is a women-only fitness and wellness studio in Khatiwala Tank, Indore. Not a pink version of a gym — a place designed from the ground up around how women actually train, recover and feel.

Why we exist

Fitness should not require armour.

Most women have a story about a gym: the unsolicited advice, the mirrors, the feeling of being watched while learning something new. ZEVA exists to remove that entire layer.

We built a studio where the only audience is other women, where asking a question is normal, and where a coach's job is to teach — not to show off. Strength training, yoga, Zumba and physiotherapy sit under one roof so your plan can change as your body and life do.

Whether you're lifting for the first time, returning after a break, managing PCOS or recovering from an injury, you'll find a starting point here that doesn't ask you to already be fit.

What we stand for

Three things we refuse to compromise on

01

Safety, not just privacy

A women-only floor is the baseline. Add coaches who ask before they correct, clear instructions and a culture where "I'm new" is a normal thing to say.

02

Strength before shortcuts

We don't sell crash diets or quick fixes. We teach progressive training, recovery and habits that survive a busy month — because that's what actually lasts.

03

Support, not comparison

No leaderboards, no before-and-after pressure. Your progress is measured against your own starting point, and celebrated quietly or loudly — your call.

The space

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A compact, well-equipped studio in the heart of Khatiwala Tank — easy to reach, easy to come back to.

01

The training floor

Racks, free weights, machines and functional kit — set up for small-group strength sessions.

02

The studio

A clear, matted space for yoga, mobility and Zumba, with room to move without bumping elbows.

03

The physio room

Private one-to-one assessments and rehab sessions, away from the main floor.

You don't have to get fit before you join. That's what we're here for.

The first visit is a conversation, not a test. We'll ask about your goals, your history and your schedule, show you around, and let you try a session with a coach nearby.

If it's not the right fit, that's completely fine — no pressure and no follow-up sales calls.
