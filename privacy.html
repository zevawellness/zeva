Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

ZEVA Women's Fitness & Wellness Studio ("ZEVA", "we", "us") runs a women-only fitness and wellness studio at Plot No. 796, Khatiwala Tank, Scheme No. 44, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452014, India. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or respond to one of our advertisements, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and to run your membership:

  • Details you give us — your name, phone number, email address, age or date of birth, and the fitness goal or programme you are interested in.
  • Enquiry messages — anything you write to us by phone call, WhatsApp, Instagram, our website enquiry form, or a lead form shown inside a Google advertisement.
  • Membership records — attendance, payments, health declarations and any physiotherapy notes you share with a coach, where you become a member.
  • Technical information — when you visit this website, our hosting provider and advertising partners may record your IP address, device type, browser, the pages you viewed and the advertisement or search term that brought you here.

2. Lead forms inside Google advertisements

Some of our Google advertisements include a lead form. When you submit that form, Google passes the information you entered (typically your name, phone number and email address) to us so that we can call or message you about your enquiry. You can also choose to let Google pre-fill those fields from your Google Account. That choice is yours and is made inside Google's interface, not ours.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use Google Ads and Google Analytics to understand which advertisements and search terms bring genuine enquiries. These services may set cookies or read device identifiers so that we can measure clicks, calls and form submissions and avoid showing the same advertisement repeatedly.

Google may use this information in line with its own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. You can control personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com, and opt out of Analytics measurement with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

4. How we use your information

  • To call, message or email you back about your enquiry, trial session or membership.
  • To confirm the current offer, price and available seats.
  • To schedule sessions and keep a record of your membership and payments.
  • To measure which advertisements work, so we spend our budget sensibly.
  • To meet our legal, tax and safety obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Who we share it with

  • Google — for advertising, measurement and lead-form delivery.
  • Our website host and communication tools — to run this site and to send and receive calls and messages.
  • Accountants and payment providers — only what is required to process membership fees and keep statutory records.
  • Authorities — where we are legally required to disclose information.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become memberships are kept for up to 24 months so that we can follow up if you ask us to. Membership and payment records are kept for as long as the law requires — currently eight years for financial records in India. You can ask us to delete your enquiry data sooner (see section 8).

7. Security

Access to enquiry and membership records is limited to ZEVA staff who need it. We use password-protected accounts and encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website and for our advertising tools. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your enquiry data;
  • stop contacting you.

Call or message +91 99818 45253 or email info.zevawellness@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

Our services are intended for women aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If a minor has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or in the law. The "last updated" date at the top will always show the current version.
