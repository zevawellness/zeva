Pricing questions

Before you ask

Why aren't prices listed on the website?
Because they genuinely change — longer memberships cost less per month, and seasonal offers come and go. Rather than show a number that's out of date, we send the current rate on WhatsApp, in writing.
Is the trial really free?
Yes. One session plus a short assessment, with no obligation to join. We'd rather you try the room before paying for it.
Can I pause my membership?
Yes — memberships include a freeze option for travel, illness or life getting in the way. Tell us in advance and we'll adjust the end date.
Do you offer shorter commitments?
We offer 3, 6 and 12-month options, plus single personal-training sessions. If you're in Indore only briefly, ask about a short-term pass.
Is there a joining fee?
No hidden joining fee. Whatever we quote is the full amount for the duration discussed — ask us to confirm it in writing.
