Good to know

Questions, answered

Is ZEVA really women-only?
Yes. Every member, guest and trainer on the floor is a woman. It's the whole point — a space where you can train without being watched or managed.
I'm a complete beginner. Will I feel out of place?
Not at all. Most women start exactly where you are. Your first session includes a short assessment, and your coach will scale every movement to your current level.
Do I need to book before visiting?
Yes, please call or WhatsApp ahead so a coach is free to show you around and take you through the trial. Walk-ins are welcome but may have to wait.
Do you offer physiotherapy and rehab?
Yes. Physiotherapy is available as one-to-one sessions for back, knee, shoulder and post-natal concerns, and it can be combined with your training plan.
Where exactly is the studio?
Plot No. 796, Khatiwala Tank, Scheme No. 44, Indore 452014 — above the Zepto store. Get directions
What should I bring to my first session?
Comfortable workout clothes, sports shoes and a water bottle. Mats and equipment are provided. Come a few minutes early so we can show you around.
